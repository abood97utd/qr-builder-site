About QR Builder

Free QR code generator that runs in your browser

QR Builder turns any link or text into a QR code you can style with custom colors, gradients and module shapes, place over a photo, view as a 3D model, and wrap in a frame such as “Scan me” or “Scan to pay”. Every design is decoded with a real scanner before you download it, so what you print will scan.

How do I make a QR code?

Paste a URL, Wi-Fi string, phone number or any text in the Content tab. The code updates instantly and the status line confirms it scans.

Can I add my logo or photo?

Yes. In the Photo tab you can place a picture behind the code, inside the dark modules, or in the centre like a logo. “Make it scannable” tunes the settings automatically.

Which download formats are available?

PNG, JPG, WEBP, SVG (vector) and PDF, from 512 up to 4096 pixels, plus direct sharing to WhatsApp, Telegram, X and email.

Is it free and private?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no server: your data and images never leave your device.
